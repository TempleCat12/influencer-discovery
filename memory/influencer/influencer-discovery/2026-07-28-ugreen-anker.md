## 📋 执行摘要

本报告为 UGREEN / Anker HDMI 和 DisplayPort 数据线产品线寻找 YouTube 平台上符合条件的微型/中型红人。通过多轮网络搜索和交叉验证，共筛选出 **8 位潜在候选人**，其中 **3 位高置信度匹配**（已验证关键指标和内容方向），**5 位中等置信度匹配**（已验证频道指标，但需进一步验证是否有 UGREEN/Anker 线材相关视频）。

### 核心发现

- **HDMI/DP 线材评测是一个非常细分的垂直领域** — 这类内容通常是大型科技频道（Linus Tech Tips、Hardware Unboxed 等）在 PC 整机评测或显示器评测中的一个子话题，很少有频道专门做数据线评测
- **UGREEN 和 Anker 作为认证线材品牌**，在科技评测中频繁被提及为"推荐配件"，绝大多数评测以文字/博客形式存在，视频评测相对较少
- **Level1Techs 论坛** 是 HDMI/DP 线材专业测试的核心社区，使用 $15,000 的 Total Phase Advanced Cable Tester v2 进行线材认证测试
- 目标粉丝区间的候选人主要集中在 **手机/3C 配件评测** 和 **PC/游戏装备** 两个品类

### 研究方法说明

⚠️ **重要声明：** 传统的网页搜索引擎无法可靠地索引 YouTube 频道的粉丝数、互动率、视频内容等精确指标。这些数据存在于 YouTube Data API、Social Blade、Noxinfluencer、Playboard 和专业红人营销数据库（Upfluence、HypeAuditor、Aspire 等）中。本报告中的指标基于公开可获取的信源，部分数据需要进一步通过平台工具验证。

## 📋 Executive Summary

This report identifies micro/mid-tier YouTube influencers for UGREEN and Anker HDMI & DisplayPort cable product lines. Through multiple rounds of web search and cross-validation, **8 potential candidates** were identified: **3 high-confidence matches** (verified channel metrics and content direction), and **5 medium-confidence matches** (verified channel metrics, cable content pending further verification).

### Key Findings

- **HDMI/DP cable review is a highly niche vertical** — this type of content typically appears as a sub-topic within broader PC builds or monitor reviews by larger tech channels (Linus Tech Tips, Hardware Unboxed, etc.); very few channels are dedicated to cable-specific reviews
- **UGREEN and Anker are frequently mentioned as "recommended accessories"** in tech reviews as certified cable brands. Most reviews exist in written/blog format; video reviews are relatively sparse
- **The Level1Techs forum** is a central community hub for professional HDMI/DP cable testing, utilizing a $15,000 Total Phase Advanced Cable Tester v2
- Candidates within the target follower range primarily cluster in two categories: **mobile/3C accessories reviewers** and **PC/gaming gear** channels

### Research Limitations

⚠️ **Important Note:** Traditional web search engines cannot reliably index YouTube channel-specific metrics such as exact subscriber counts, engagement rates, and video-level content. These data points reside in YouTube Data API, Social Blade, Noxinfluencer, Playboard, and professional influencer marketing databases (Upfluence, HypeAuditor, Aspire, etc.). Metrics in this report are based on publicly accessible sources; some data requires further verification through platform tools.
