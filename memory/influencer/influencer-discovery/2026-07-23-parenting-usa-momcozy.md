## Step 1 — Discovery Parameters

**Brand/Product**: Momcozy / maternal care & baby gear brands

**Search Criteria**:

| Parameter | Requirement | Priority |

| Follower Range | 50K–200K | Required |
| Engagement Rate | >4% | Required |
| Location | USA | Required |
| Language | English | Required |
| Content Type | Video (Reels/YT), photo carousels, reviews | Preferred |
| Posting Frequency | ≥2 posts/week | Preferred |
| Audience Demographics | Parents, expecting mothers, 25–40 F primary | Preferred |
| Brand Safety | No controversial content, no competitor exclusivity | Required |

**Nice-to-Have**:
- Previous work with maternal/baby brands (breast pumps, baby carriers, postpartum care)
- Nursing/lactation/child development credentials
- Multi-platform presence
- Authentic, review-style content (not polished ads)

**Exclusions**:
- Exclusivity contracts with competitor brands (Willow, Elvie, Medela, Spectra)
- Family vlog channels exploiting children (post-Ruby Franke sensitivity)
- Political/controversial content creators
- Inactive accounts (>30 days no posting)

- "Honest review" and "real mom" content generates higher purchase intent than polished ads
- Pumping/breastfeeding content has surged post-pandemic as more moms return to work and need hands-free solutions
- Dad POV content is an underutilized angle in maternal care (gift-giving, partner support)

**Competitive Picture**:
- Momcozy's known ambassadors: Abbie Herbert (macro, 2.6M IG), Becca Kufrin (TV personality), Jenna Dewan (actress) — all celebrity-tier
- Mid-tier and micro-tier influencer strategy appears underdeveloped relative to competitors

- BabyBuddha leverages aggressive discount-code affiliate strategy with micro-influencers
- Momcozy has an affiliate program (10-15% commission) — could be leveraged for micro-tier partnerships

**Untapped Opportunities**:
- **Lactation consultant/nurse niche**: Momcozy has no visible partnerships here; @karrie_locher is an open opportunity with highest possible audience overlap

- **Postpartum doula community**: Underleveraged by all pump brands; @glowmaven could pioneer this category
